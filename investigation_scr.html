PART ONE: A VACANCY, AN UNAUTHORIZED ELECTION, AND AN ATTORNEY'S CONFESSION

In May 2025, Stone Creek Ranch completed the "Turnover" — the formal handoff from developer control to homeowner control. When the developer stepped down, two Directors remained: Kenneth Wilson and a Director named Debbie. One Board seat was now vacant.

The governing bylaws are clear. Article III, Section 8 states vacancies "may be filled by a majority of the remaining Directors." The two remaining Board members had full authority to simply appoint someone. No election required.

Sterling Property Management told them an election was required. That direction was wrong. Sterling's authority under the bylaws — Article III, Section 22 — is limited entirely to "duties and services as the Board shall authorize." No provision authorizes Sterling to direct the Board to hold an election.

The Association's own legal counsel — Cliff Davis, Esq. — later confirmed it in writing.

"The problem is that there should not have been a Board member election in May 2025. If an election was held in May 2025, it was in violation of the Bylaws."

"If anything, Eddie should have been appointed by the Board to fill the unexpired term of the vacant position."

"If the election was held at a Board meeting in May 2025, it was not proper as a Board member election cannot take place at a Board meeting. Only a Board member appointment can take place at a Board meeting."

— Cliff Davis, Esq., Legal Counsel, Stone Creek Ranch Community Association, Inc. · Exhibit C · Permanent Official Association Record

Sterling organized and administered a new Board election — distributing official ballot instructions with individually assigned Voter IDs and Voter Keys to every Stone Creek Ranch household. Three candidates were listed: Edward Barnot, Danielle Smith, and Shantesa Terrell.

The official Board Meeting Notice — Exhibit F — listed under New Business: "Announcement of the new Board member." Director Barnot's own candidate biography — Exhibit G — stated: "I am seeking reelection and asking for your vote once again."
